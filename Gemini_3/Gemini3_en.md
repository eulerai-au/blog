### 2) Build: Create Any Project

Building on the success of 2.5 Pro, Gemini 3 fulfills its promise to turn any developer idea into reality. It excels at zero-shot generation, handles complex prompts and instructions, and renders richer, more interactive web UIs.

Gemini 3 is also Google's best Vibe Coding model to date. It tops the WebDev Arena leaderboard with an impressive 1487 Elo score. It achieved 54.2% on the Terminal-Bench 2.0 test (evaluating tool-use capability via terminal operations), and significantly outperformed 2.5 Pro on the SWE-bench Verified test (which measures coding agent performance), scoring **76.2%**.

Gemini 3 is available via Google AI Studio, Vertex AI, Gemini CLI, and Google Antigravity (Google’s newly released Agent AI IDE). It has also been integrated into third-party ecosystems such as Cursor, GitHub, JetBrains, Manus, and Replit.

### 3) Plan: Organize Anything

Beyond coding, Gemini 3 has improved reliability in planning tasks, including:

* **Long-Term Planning Stability**: Ranked first in Vending-Bench 2 (a one-year business simulation), consistently using tools and decision-making with stability and higher returns.
* **Real-World Task Execution**: Capable of handling multi-step workflows like booking local services or organizing emails under user authorization and review.

## Onboarding Paths for Different Users

Gemini 3 is not just a more powerful "Q&A model"—it’s a step toward an AI that perceives, reasons, and acts. It forms a closed loop across Learn-Build-Plan, making the concept of an "AI collaborator" a reality. It supports various user needs, such as:

* **Students/Self-learners**: Feed it papers or online courses to auto-generate flashcards and visualizations; use the generative UI in Search AI to learn complex mechanisms (e.g., RNA polymerase).
* **Creators/Designers**: Use multimodality and long context for stylized visualizations, storyboards, and interactive prototypes.
* **Developers**: Engage in vibe coding in AI Studio/Antigravity with agents for end-to-end prototyping; deploy production-grade models with Vertex AI.
* **Professionals/Business Ops**: Delegate multi-step processes (e.g., inbox cleanup, service bookings, document organization) to intelligent agents—users just need to define goals and review results.
